Refund Policy

Effective: 28 September 2026

Refund eligibility depends on the service purchased and work already performed.

Before work begins

If a customer cancels before substantive project work begins, Mobicom will assess the payment for refund after deducting any disclosed, non-recoverable third-party costs already incurred for that customer.

Work already performed

Where work has started, amounts relating to completed work, approved milestones, committed resources or non-recoverable third-party purchases may not be refundable. Any remaining refundable amount will be assessed fairly against the agreed project scope and applicable law.

Faulty or incorrectly supplied services

Please notify us promptly if a deliverable materially differs from the agreed scope. We will investigate and, where appropriate, correct the deliverable or provide another remedy required by applicable law.

Refund method

Approved refunds are returned using an appropriate payment method and may require transaction verification. Processing times can depend on the payment provider and bank.
